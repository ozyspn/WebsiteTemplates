The morning

Fog rolled in off the Pacific at 6am and didn't lift until the ceremony. Lena got ready in a cabin that smelled like coffee and eucalyptus, her sister steaming the dress while her dad pretended not to tear up by the window. Nobody asked me to pose anything. I just kept the shutter close.

The person behind the camera

Hi, I'm
Jacob.

I started shooting weddings in 2014 because I'm nosy and I cry easily — turns out that's the whole job. I'm less interested in the perfectly-lit kiss than in the ninety seconds before it, when nobody's ready and everything's real.

I work quietly, in two pairs of hands, and I'll never make your great-aunt do a jumping photo. I'm based in San Francisco and I'll happily get on a plane — about a third of my year happens somewhere with a worse cell signal and a better view.

When I'm not shooting I'm developing film in a closet, losing at chess, and over-tipping baristas.
